Fish Hunter Privacy Policy

Welcome to Fish Hunter. Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how the Fish Hunter mobile application handles information and user data.

Effective Date: May 15, 2026
Overview

1. Information We Collect

Fish Hunter &&&&&&& is designed to work completely offline and does not require account registration or sign-in.

The app stores gameplay-related information locally on your device using on-device storage technology (Hive), including:

  • Coins
  • Fish inventory
  • Upgrades
  • Unlocked areas
  • Selected area
  • Daily reward status
  • Music preference

We do not collect or store personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
Usage

2. How We Use Information

The locally stored gameplay data is used only to:

  • Save your game progress
  • Restore your game state when reopening the app
  • Support core gameplay functionality and features

Your data remains on your device and is not used for advertising or user tracking.

Sharing

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

Because gameplay data is stored locally on your device, it is not transmitted to our servers by default.

Third Parties

4. Ads, Analytics, and Third-Party Services

Fish Hunter does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not run third-party analytics services inside the app.

However, the app may rely on platform or framework components such as:

  • Flutter runtime
  • Google Play Store or App Store distribution services

These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Permissions

5. Permissions

Fish Hunter does not request sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Precise location

These permissions are not required for the core gameplay experience.

Retention

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your gameplay data remains stored on your device until:

  • You clear the app data, or
  • You uninstall the application

Please note that uninstalling the app may permanently remove locally stored game progress.

Children

7. Children's Privacy

Fish Hunter does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

8. Security

We apply reasonable technical measures to protect locally stored data within the app. However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Updates

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any future updates will be reflected by revising the Effective Date at the top of this page.
